# Final Exam Guidelines

## Submission Requirements

### Paper Format

- **Springer LaTeX format**
- **Maximum 6 pages**
- **Written fully in English** (including figures, labels, tables, and captions)

### Figures and Visualizations

- **No screenshots accepted**
- **Graphs/diagrams generated by GenAI are not accepted**
- Use proper drawing and visualization tools
- All figures must include:
  - Figure number
  - Caption
  - Clear labels
  - Readable text

### Presentation

- **5-minute pitch**
- No slides required
- Present directly from the exported PDF
- External reviewers may ask questions about your project

You are expected to clearly explain:

- The environmental problem addressed
- Why the problem matters
- Your Computer Vision pipeline
- Why you selected your methods
- Key decisions and trade-offs
- Main results and limitations

### Submission

Upload to Moodle:

1. **Final Paper (.pdf)**
2. **Source Files (.zip)** including:
   - Jupyter notebook(s)
   - Code
   - Figures
   - Supporting files (.zip)

# Important Grading Notes

### Common Penalties

The following issues may significantly reduce the grade:

- Using **screenshots** in the paper
- Using **AI-generated diagrams or graphs**
- Figures without captions or unreadable labels
- Text, labels, or figures **not written in English**
- Undefined abbreviations or unexplained technical terms
- Showing results **without interpretation**
- Using only **2–3 references**
- Missing notebook/code in the submission
- Poor-quality visualizations

### Expectations Compared to the Midterm

This final project is **not a first prototype**.

Students are expected to demonstrate:

- Improved methodology
- Better justification of decisions
- More complete implementation
- Stronger experimental results
- Better scientific communication
- Improvements based on midterm feedback

Projects showing little progress from the midterm may receive reduced scores even if technically functional.
