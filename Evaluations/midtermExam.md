# Midterm Challenge: Satellite Computer Vision for Environmental Impact

## Overview

Your task is to design and implement a Computer Vision pipeline to analyze satellite imagery and address a real environmental problem.

This midterm covers Units 1–4 and will evolve into your Final Project.

---

## Objective

Design a Computer Vision pipeline that uses satellite imagery to solve a specific, real-world environmental problem.

Your solution must clearly define:

- What problem you are solving  
- Why it matters  
- How your pipeline provides insight or value  

---

## Example Problem Directions

You must define your own specific version:

- Detect deforestation over time in a specific region  
- Analyze changes in water bodies (e.g., shrinking rivers or lakes)  
- Identify environmental damage (fires, erosion, human activity)  
- Monitor vegetation health or land-use change  

Important:  
Do not perform tasks like “segment water vs land” unless it is part of a clear applied objective.

---

## Suggested Data Sources

You may use any dataset, but here are recommended options:

### Easy / Fast Setup
- Kaggle satellite image datasets  
- Google Earth Engine (exported images)  
- Public “before vs after” datasets (deforestation, disasters, etc.)

### Intermediate
- Sentinel-2 imagery  
- Landsat datasets  

### Advanced (Optional)
- AlphaEarth (for embeddings and advanced representations)

---

## Technical Requirements

Your pipeline must include:

1. Image acquisition  
2. Preprocessing  
   - Example: resizing, filtering, normalization, etc.  
3. Feature extraction or transformation  
4. Model or decision logic  
   - Classical approach OR deep learning-based (transfer learning allowed)  
5. Visualization and interpretation of results
